1. Pick your source

Paste a YouTube URL or a local video file path. The cutting itself runs locally with clipper.py — this page does not cut video in your browser, and it does not pretend to.

python3 clipper.py "PASTE_URL_OR_FILE_HERE" --count 7 --length 60 --output ./output

⚠️ Permission still matters. This tool cuts faster — it does not grant permission. Only use it on creators who have given permission to clip their videos (e.g. CarryMinati, per Uzair's check) or on campaigns that grant permission on joining.

2. Review the moments it found

After the CLI finishes, it writes clips.json in the output folder. Load that file here to preview the moments, tick the ones you want to keep, and copy their titles.


  


  

    
How it works (honest version)

    
In the browser (this page): builds the command, and previews
       clips.json results. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is cut here.

    
On your computer (clipper.py): downloads the source with
       yt-dlp (YouTube URLs only), analyses the audio with ffmpeg — loudness
       peaks, silence breaks, and an optional local faster-whisper transcript —
       scores every 60-second window, picks the top N with no overlap, and cuts
       them to vertical 1080×1920 with a blurred background, H.264 + AAC, no watermark.

    
What it does well: loud reactions, laughter, shouting, hype moments,
       shootouts and chases.

    
What it misses: silent visual moments, visual gags, and jokes that
       only work from the transcript. Always preview before uploading.

    
Requirements: ffmpeg, ffprobe, yt-dlp on PATH. Python 3.10+, stdlib only.
       faster-whisper is optional.
