That YouTube link couldn't be fetched right now

Our server is free, so it sleeps after 15 minutes with no use, and YouTube sometimes blocks it — that is why the link failed. It is not a problem with your video.

What to do instead — it takes about a minute:

  1. Download the video to your device first — for example with yt-dlp, or a video downloader of your choice.
  2. Come back here and upload that video file above (drop it on the box).
  3. Pick how many clips and what size, press Generate Clips, and download the clips.

An uploaded file never leaves your device — the cutting happens in your browser. A pasted link is different: our server has to fetch that video first, then hand the file to your browser to cut.

How it works

1. Upload your video file — or paste a YouTube link, which our own free server fetches first. If it can't fetch it, upload the file instead.
2. Pick how many clips, how long, and what size.
3. Press Generate Clips. The tool listens to the audio, finds the loudest, most energetic moments, and cuts each clip in your browser.
4. Watch every clip in the preview, then download the ones you like.

Honest limits: it is best at loud reactions, laughter and chases, and can miss silent visual jokes. It ranks moments by energy, not by how funny they are — so always watch a clip before you upload it. It also does not grant permission to clip anyone's video: only use it on creators who have given permission to clip their videos.

First run loads the video cutter (about 30 MB) from the internet, so it can take a minute on a slow connection. After that, cutting happens on your device. Very long or very large videos (over about 1 GB) can be slow or run out of browser memory — shorter videos work best.
